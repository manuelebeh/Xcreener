The code licenses ([AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) and MIT in the directories that carry their own `LICENSE`) cover the source code. They do not grant any right to the Xcreener name or brand. Section 7(e) of the AGPL allows this explicitly.

## What this policy covers

- The name "Xcreener" and names confusingly similar to it.
- The Xcreener logo, app icons and visual identity.

## You may

- Say that your project is "based on Xcreener" or "a fork of Xcreener".
- Refer to Xcreener by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Redistribute official, unmodified Xcreener releases under their original name.

## You may not

- Publish a modified version, fork or derived product under the Xcreener name or logo. Rename it and replace the icons first.
- Use Xcreener in a product name, company name, domain name or app store listing.
- Suggest that your project is official or endorsed by Xcreener when it is not.
