# Trademark policy

The code licenses ([AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) and MIT in the directories that carry their own `LICENSE`) cover the source code. They do not grant any right to the SnapGlow name or brand. Section 7(e) of the AGPL allows this explicitly.

## What this policy covers

- The name "SnapGlow" and names confusingly similar to it.
- The SnapGlow logo, app icons and visual identity.
- The `snapglow.app` domain.

## You may

- Say that your project is "based on SnapGlow" or "a fork of SnapGlow".
- Refer to SnapGlow by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Redistribute official, unmodified SnapGlow releases under their original name.

## You may not

- Publish a modified version, fork or derived product under the SnapGlow name or logo. Rename it and replace the icons first.
- Use SnapGlow in a product name, company name, domain name or app store listing.
- Suggest that your project is official or endorsed by SnapGlow when it is not.
