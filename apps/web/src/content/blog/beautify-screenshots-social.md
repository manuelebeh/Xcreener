Social screenshots need consistent padding and backgrounds. Here's a fast workflow with SnapGlow.

## 1. Capture the region

Use `CmdOrControl+Shift+2` (customizable) to capture any window or zone.

## 2. Pick a social preset

Choose **Twitter/X**, **LinkedIn**, or **Instagram** from the preset bar — aspect ratio and padding adjust automatically.

## 3. Apply a background

Gradient presets like **Midnight Code** or **Sunset** add depth without opening a design tool.

## 4. Export or share

Export PNG/WebP locally, or upload to cloud for a share link when you're ready.

Browse the [template marketplace](/marketplace) for community presets.
