Share Xcreener with your audience and earn **30% commission** on each referred customer's first purchase.

## How it works

1. Sign up at the [affiliate portal](/affiliates).
2. Share your unique referral link (`?via=your-code`).
3. Track clicks and conversions in your Rewardful dashboard.
4. Receive payouts automatically via Stripe.

## Attribution window

Referrals are tracked for **90 days** via browser cookie after the first click.

## Questions

Contact **affiliates@xcreener.app** for custom partnerships or higher-tier deals.
