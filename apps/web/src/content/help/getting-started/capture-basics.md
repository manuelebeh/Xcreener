# Capture basics

SnapGlow runs from your menu bar or system tray. Captures stay on your device until you export or upload.

## Global shortcut

Set your shortcut in **Settings → Global capture shortcut** (default `CmdOrControl+Shift+Digit2`).

## Capture modes

- **Region** — drag to select an area
- **Window** — click a window
- **Screen** — full display
- **Scrolling** — stitch a long page (where supported)

## After capture

The editor opens with beautify presets, annotations, redaction, and export. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud unless you explicitly share or sync to your library.
