# Billing and plans

| Plan | Highlights |
|------|------------|
| **Free** | 1 member, personal cloud |
| **Team Starter** | Up to 5 members, brand kit, collaboration |
| **Team Pro** | Unlimited seats, API keys, SSO |

## Upgrade

Admins click **Upgrade to Team Starter** or **Team Pro** in Settings. Checkout opens in the browser via Stripe.

## Manage subscription

After subscribing, use **Manage subscription** to open the Stripe Customer Portal — update seats, payment method, or cancel.

## SSO (Team Pro)

Enable SSO from **Billing & SSO** to create an Auth0 Organization for your workspace.
