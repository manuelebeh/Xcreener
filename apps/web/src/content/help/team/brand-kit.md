# Brand kit

Paid team plans include a shared brand kit applied to exports when enabled.

## Configure

In **Settings → Team → Brand kit**:

- Upload a logo (stored securely in cloud storage)
- Set primary/secondary colors and fonts
- Configure watermark text, URL, position, and opacity

## Enable on export

Toggle **Use team brand kit** in Settings. When a paid workspace is active, exports merge the team kit over your personal watermark.

## Caching

The desktop app caches brand kits locally for faster exports offline.
