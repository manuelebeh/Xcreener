# Invitations and roles

Admins create invite links from **Settings → Team → Invite member**.

## Invite flow

1. Optionally restrict the invite to an email address
2. Choose a role (admin, editor, viewer)
3. Copy the link — it opens `snapglow://team/invite?token=…` in SnapGlow

Invitees sign in with Auth0 if needed, then join the workspace automatically.

## Changing roles

Admins can update or remove members from the members list. On a paid plan, adding, removing, or promoting an editor or admin updates the seat count, prorated. Viewers are free.

## Limits

Free workspaces allow one member. **Team Starter** supports five editors or admins and any number of viewers; **Team Pro** is unlimited.
